DEGIRO: choose your Transactions.csv and Account.csv exports together (Inbox → Transacties and Inbox → Rekeningoverzicht, from the day you opened the account). They are read as they are, and daily market prices are added through your Interactive Brokers connector when it is available.

Other brokers: paste a CSV or choose a file. Columns: date, type, ticker, quantity, price, amount, fee, name, category. Types: deposit, withdrawal, buy, sell, dividend, interest, fee and price (a quote for a date, used to value open positions). Comma or semicolon separated, Dutch decimals are fine.

Return

Current portfolio value iOpen positions at their latest known price, plus available cash.
Total PnL iChange in value minus money you deposited or withdrew in this period. Equals unrealized + realized + income − fees.
Period return iTime-weighted return counts every day equally, whatever amount was invested: it shows how your choices performed. Money-weighted return weighs each period by the money you had in, so it shows what your euros actually earned. They differ when big gains or losses happen while the account is small.
Unrealized PnL iGain or loss on positions you still hold, against your average purchase price including fees.
Realized PnL iProfit or loss locked in by sales in this period, after trading costs.
Net income iDividends and interest received in this period, minus account fees.
Available cash iUninvested cash in the account, and the net amount you have deposited over the full history.
